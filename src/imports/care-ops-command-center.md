Design a Care Operations Command Center for a healthcare platform managing home health and hospice agencies.

The command center should provide a centralized view of operational issues and daily workload.

Layout Zones

Critical Issues

Display alert cards for urgent operational problems such as:

Delayed visits
EVV transmission failures
Missing physician signatures
Authorization exceeded
Admissions not ready

Each card should display the number of affected items and provide a quick action to open the relevant queue.

Today's Operations

Display panels showing the current operational status including:

Visits scheduled today
Visits in progress
Visits completed
Open shifts

Operational Queues

Display lists of actionable work items including:

Admissions needing completion
Delayed visits
EVV errors
Missing documentation
QA returned documents
Claims ready for submission

Each queue item should display patient name, admission date, assigned staff member, issue description, and priority.

Operational Insights

Display lightweight summary metrics such as:

Admissions today
Visits completed today
Claims ready for billing
Authorizations expiring soon
Hospice assessments due

The command center should prioritize operational awareness and quick problem resolution.
