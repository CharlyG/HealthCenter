Design the Admission Overview screen as a Care Episode Dashboard for the selected patient admission.

The purpose of this screen is to give staff an immediate understanding of the status of the patient's episode of care.

Layout Structure

Admission Context Header

Display:

Admission start date
Admission status
Primary payer
Disciplines involved
Case manager
Authorization status
Certification period
Episode progress indicator

Zone 1 — Episode Status

Display key admission metrics:

Visits scheduled this week
Visits completed
Visits missing documentation
Authorization usage
Certification progress

Zone 2 — Care Team

Display assigned care team members:

Primary nurse
Therapists
Social worker
Case manager
Medical director

Provide quick actions to message or reassign team members.

Zone 3 — Upcoming Visits

Display next scheduled visits with:

date
discipline
caregiver
visit status

Allow quick access to visit documentation.

Zone 4 — Documentation Status

Display progress for required documentation such as:

Plans of care
Visit notes
Orders
Assessments (OASIS / HOPE)

Highlight missing or overdue documentation.

Zone 5 — Authorization Tracker

Display authorization limits and usage including:

Total authorized visits
Visits completed
Remaining visits

Highlight when the authorization is close to being exceeded.

Zone 6 — Alerts

Display admission-level alerts such as:

Missing documentation
Delayed visits
Authorization issues
EVV errors

The Care Episode Dashboard must allow staff to understand the admission status at a glance.
